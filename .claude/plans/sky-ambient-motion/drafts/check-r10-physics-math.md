### PHY10-1 (major). H8's new desktop bar fails by design at 1024 × 768: P9's fallback flights fall under the planner's 160 px minimum below about 1,280 px of width, so about 11% of launches there fail every steer

- **Section.** 6.10, the steer at a start point, step 7 (`PLAN.md:2541`), and the flight block's doc (`:2451`); H8 (`:2901`); P9 step 2 (`:3243`), which needs H8's launch parts green.
- **Problem.**

  - The cause is arithmetic. P9 removes cf4d7b8's speed clamp (`METEOR.speed` [240, 460] and `speedShare`, `meteors.ts:195-196`) and takes speeds from the window: a 1024 px window gives 15.5 px per degree at its centre (focal 886.8 px). The fallback pass flies the class's durations × 0.64, so its nominal lengths, before the rate, jitter and range factors, are at most 125 (slow), 153 (medium) and 174 px (fast), against the planner's own 160 px minimum, which step 7 keeps (`otherwise null, so the planner keeps its own minimum (160 px, or 110 on narrow screens)`). At 1,280 px the slow class still tops out at 156 px; on an 844 px-tall phone at 103, 126 and 144 px against 110. cf4d7b8's fallback reached its minimum through the 240 px/s floor: 240 × [0.88, 1.12] × [0.65, 0.9] s gives 137 to 242 px.
  - So the pass that the flight block's new doc keeps "where no long flight fits (a short laptop window, a phone)" rarely produces a flight on small desktop windows, while the copy there leaves little room for long ones. An implementer reaches P9 step 2, runs H8 on the archived layouts, and sees the bar fail with no guidance on whether the code or the plan is wrong.
- **Evidence.**
  - The archived veil batch (`results/batch/planner-9-viewports.txt`, proto row): cf4d7b8 plans 99.2% of 1024 × 768 attempts with one fresh radiant each, 22.3% of them short fallbacks.
  - The P9 prototype (`p9proto.mjs`, 6,000 to 8,000 launches a run, four runs): at 1024 × 768 11.1 to 11.6% of launches fail every steer (4 steers), and only 4.1 to 4.3% of planned flights come from the fallback pass. Without `clearOf` it is still 9.8% (`p9diag.mjs`). Attempt outcomes at 1024 × 768 over 3,000 launches: 271,679 long-pass candidates under 160 px, 202,508 fallback candidates under 160 px, 285,259 veil rejections, 151,839 past the right edge, 2,668 accepted.

- **Fix.** Prefer the design fix, in I2's existing seam (`Course.minPath`, so `planFlight` gains nothing): 6.10's step 7 reads "`minPath = RADIANTS.nearRadiantPath` when ...; otherwise, on the fallback pass, the planner's own minimum (`METEOR.path[0]`, or `METEOR.narrowMinPath` on narrow screens) × `METEOR.fallbackScale` (102 and 70 px), because that pass's flights are that much shorter and P9 drops cf4d7b8's 240 px/s speed floor, which kept its fallback flights near 160 px [computed, check report]; otherwise null". The alternative, if 160 px streaks must stay: H8's bar reads "under 5% on each desktop layout 1,280 px wide or more, and about 12% at 1024 × 768, where the fallback pass rarely fits [computed, check report]", and 6.10 says the fallback serves only wider windows.
