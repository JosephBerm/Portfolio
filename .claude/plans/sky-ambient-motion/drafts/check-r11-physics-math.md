## Findings

Two minor findings. No blocking or major ones.

### PHY11-1 (minor). Two `METEOR` docs still state the unscaled minimum path after PHY10-1's fix: the narrow block's rewritten doc says "paths at least `narrowMinPath` px", and `path`'s kept doc gives the range as 160 to 640 px

- **Section.** 6.10, "The inner docs of the keys that stay are rewritten" (`PLAN.md:2455`), against "Inside `planFlight`" (`:2609`) and the steer's step 7 (`:2544`).
- **Problem.** Round 10 made the fallback pass accept paths down to `minPath × METEOR.fallbackScale`: 102 px on desktop and 70 px on narrow screens. The plan rewrites the narrow block's doc in P9 to "Narrow screens: paths at least `narrowMinPath` px and at most `narrowMaxPathShare` of the width", which is false on the fallback pass (70 px, where about two in five planned phone flights fly). `METEOR.path`'s doc, which the plan keeps (cf4d7b8 `config.ts:296`, "Path length range (px)", `[160, 640]`), now misstates the shortest accepted path the same way. The flight block's new doc (`:2454`, "accepts paths that much shorter") is right, so the config block contradicts itself, and a tuner reading the narrow block would expect no flight under 110 px on a phone.
- **Evidence.** `:2609`: "`minPath = course.minPath ?? minPath × (fallback ? METEOR.fallbackScale : 1)` ... (1.5% with the scaled minimum: 102 px, 70 on narrow screens)"; `:2544`: "the planner keeps its own minimum (160 px, or 110 on narrow screens, times `METEOR.fallbackScale` on the fallback pass)"; `:2455` as quoted; cf4d7b8 `config.ts:296-298` and `meteors.ts:197`. Round 10's prototype puts 37.9 to 41.4% of planned 390 × 844 flights on the fallback pass (`check-r10-physics/p9proto-results.txt`).
- **Fix.** The narrow block's doc reads "Narrow screens: paths at least `narrowMinPath` px (`fallbackScale` times that on the fallback pass) and at most `narrowMaxPathShare` of the width." Add `path` (`:296`) to the rewritten docs: "Path length range (px; the fallback pass accepts `fallbackScale` times the shortest); the longest is also at most `pathShare` of the longer viewport side."

### PHY11-2 (minor). AC-37's "fireballs at 4% of launches" reads as a realized share, which P10's design puts near 3%

- **Section.** AC-37 (`PLAN.md:3462`); P10's visible change (`:3281`); E7 (`:147`).
- **Problem.** P10 draws a fireball with `chance` 0.04 per launch that is eligible (desktop, at least 20 s after the reveal, at least 120 s after the last fireball; `:3265`, and the `METEOR_KINDS` doc says "odds per launch", `:3270`). At about 4.8 launches a minute, the 120 s spacing makes the next 9 to 10 launches ineligible after every fireball, so fireballs are about 3.1% of all launches over a visit's first ten minutes. AC-37's pass criterion, checked by H7, says "fireballs at 4% of launches"; a harness that counts the share of launches that are fireballs fails it by design. The rate wording is borderline too: with the long-visit stretch the visitor sees about one every 8 minutes over the first 5 and one every 10 over the first 10, as E7 says, but the mean interval never reaches the "5" end (120 s plus 1 / (0.04 × 4.74 a minute) = 7.3 minutes at best), and it grows to about 13 to 14 minutes over 20 to 30 minutes.
- **Evidence** (`rates.mjs`, 20,000 visits each, every fireball plan fitting, as at 1440 × 900, where P9's prototype flies 6.6% fireballs from the 8% per-candidate draw, so fireball candidates fit about 81% as often as plain ones and a forced fireball plan almost always finds one among its up to 640 long-pass candidates): realized fireball share of launches over 10 minutes 3.08%; expected fireballs 0.62, 0.98, 1.58 and 2.18 in the first 5, 10, 20 and 30 minutes (one per 8.1, 10.2, 12.7 and 13.8 minutes); a fireball in 11.9% of first minutes, so AC-37's "about 12%" holds.
- **Fix.** AC-37: "from P10, fireballs drawn at 4% odds per eligible launch (about 3% of all launches, given the spacing), at least 120 s apart and none before 20 s, in about 12% of first minutes (E7)". P10's visible change: "about one every 8 to 10 minutes over a visit's first 5 to 10, rarer as gaps stretch [computed, check report]". E7 may keep its target or read "about 1 per 8 to 10 minutes early in a visit".
